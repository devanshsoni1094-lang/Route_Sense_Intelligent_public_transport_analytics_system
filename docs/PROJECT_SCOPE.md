# PROJECT SCOPE & ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

**Platform:** Intelligent Public Transport Analytics Platform (IPTAP)  
**Pilot Authority:** Bengaluru Metropolitan Transport Corporation (BMTC)  
**Budget Allocation:** ₹2,00,000  

---

## 1. Executive Deliverables

IPTAP is commissioned as an operational decision-support platform for municipal corporations, government transport departments, public bus operators, and urban mobility planners in India.

### Core Modules Delivered:
1. Executive Intelligence Dashboard
2. Live Operations Command Centre
3. Route Performance Analytics
4. Passenger Demand Analytics
5. Delay Prediction & Demand Forecasting (MLOps)
6. Service Reliability & Anomaly Detection
7. Route & Service Optimization Recommendations
8. Interactive Geographic Intelligence (GIS)
9. Data Ingestion & Data Quality Centre
10. Reports & Exports Generator
11. User, Role & Administration Management

---

## 2. Operating Assumptions & Environment Modes

- **DEMO MODE**: Clearly labeled `DEMO DATA` status indicator rendered when synthetic/simulated feeds are active.
- **CONNECTED MODE**: Enabled when verified GTFS or live AVL feeds are authenticated and bound to backend adapters.
- **PRODUCTION MODE**: Ready for hosting on municipal infrastructure with PostgreSQL/PostGIS.
